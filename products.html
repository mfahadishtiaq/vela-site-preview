Capture · CRM with AI

Compass

Your enquiries, followed up for you.

  • A CRM shaped to your business

    Built around your stages, your forms and your words, not a template you have to work around.

  • You own all of it

    It runs in your own accounts. We never hold your client data, and the access we use to look after it can’t read it.

  • Intelligence built in

    AI brings forward what matters: a morning brief, client emails written and sent for you, and Helm to answer questions about any client.

Part of Broker Desk

Organise · Document management

Harbour

Your paperwork, filed for you.

  • Filed as it arrives

    Documents from email or WhatsApp are recognised, renamed and saved to the right client folder.

  • Know what’s missing

    Each client has a checklist that ticks itself as documents come in.

  • Follow-ups handled

    Pending documents, reminders and follow-ups go to your clients automatically, so every file stays on schedule.

Part of Broker Desk

Find · Information retrieval

Lighthouse

The right information, found when it’s needed.

  • Searches what you keep

    Documents, emails and notes across the systems you use, all in one search.

  • Every result has a source

    It points to the page it came from, so anyone can check it in a click.

  • Only what each person may see

    It follows the permissions you already have, person by person.

Assist · Personal AI assistant

Helm

The one you talk to. It knows what the others are doing.

  • Ask in plain words

    In your email, your team chat or on your phone, like asking someone in the office.

  • Knows what the others are doing

    It checks Compass, Harbour and Lighthouse for you and brings back the answer.

  • Set up for your business

    Set up in your own accounts, with your words and your rules.
