How a system works

You talk to one. The rest work behind it.

Helm, your personal AI assistant, is the only one you speak to. Behind it, three systems run your office and keep Helm up to date.

  • Compass takes every enquiry and follows it up.
  • Harbour files every document and keeps each checklist.
  • Lighthouse finds the information you keep, with its source.
  • Helm asks them for you and brings back the answer.

Example
  1. 7:30Compass

    Your morning brief: three new enquiries overnight, two files waiting on documents, one deal closing this week.

  2. 7:31Compass

    Each new enquiry gets a reply from your own email, and its follow-ups are set.

  3. 9:14Harbour

    A payslip arrives by email. It is renamed, filed to the client’s folder, and the checklist moves to 7 of 8.

  4. 10:00Harbour

    A reminder goes to the client for the missing bank statement.

  5. 11:20Helm

    You ask Which files are still missing documents? Helm checks Harbour and lists them.

  6. 14:45Lighthouse

    Helm needs the office policy on late documents. Lighthouse finds it, page and source attached.

  7. 16:30Compass

    Yesterday’s enquiry replies to its follow-up, and the lead moves to Documents.
